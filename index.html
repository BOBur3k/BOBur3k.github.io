Data Science

My data science journey began with The Knowledge House's immersive 400+ hour project-based curriculum, sparking my passion for extracting insights from data. I honed skills in Python, statistical analysis, data visualization, and handling large datasets. Developing proficiency in SQL, Tableau, NumPy, pandas, and scikit-learn, I applied advanced techniques like regression, clustering, ANOVA, time series analyses, and both unsupervised and supervised machine learning algorithms. During the program, I worked on over 10 projects that increased my skills in data science and helped me become a rising data scientist professional.

I am well-equipped to tackle complex data challenges, extract valuable insights, and drive impactful data-driven strategies.

Higher Education

I began my career at Borough of Manhattan Community College, providing guidance to students, helping them understand and love Math. Later, I advanced to become a pre-college advisor at both Guttman Community College and New York City College of Technology. Currently, I focus on supporting incoming freshmen students at Guttman CC, where I assist with their transition to college through the Math Start program. Additionally, I prepare high schoolers for college readiness via a 12-week self-developed workshop series called “Intro to Planning and Success in College.” This initiative is part of both the FRNYC and College Now programs at CUNY.

Through tutoring, advising, and FYS, I hope to contribute to the great change in higher education.

Digital Marketing
